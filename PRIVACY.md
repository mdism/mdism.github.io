# Privacy policy: free tools at tools.shaikhismail.com

Live page: https://tools.shaikhismail.com/privacy.html

## The short version

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your web browser. Your files and the numbers you type are never uploaded to any server, never stored and never shared. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

## Temporary processing

If a tool has to hold something while it works (for example the pages being combined into one PDF), it is held only in your browser's memory for that tab, and it is deleted as soon as processing finishes, when you clear the list, or when you close or reload the tab.

## Each tool

Every tool has its own privacy page with the details:

Image Compressor: Shrink JPG, PNG and WebP files right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
PDF Merge & Split: Combine PDFs into one, or pull out the pages you need.
Exam Photo Resizer: Resize photos and signatures to exact KB and pixel sizes for exam forms.
GST, EMI & SIP Calculator: Quick GST, EMI and SIP calculations with clear breakdowns.

## What is stored on your device

Only your light or dark theme choice, and only if you use the toggle. It stays in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

## Bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

To keep bots and spammers out, each tool asks for a quick Cloudflare Turnstile check before it unlocks. Cloudflare looks at technical signals from your browser, such as your IP address and browser details, to tell people from bots. It never sees your files or anything you type. Our small verification service, which also runs on Cloudflare, only passes the one-time token to Cloudflare for a yes or no and keeps nothing. Cloudflare handles its part under the Cloudflare privacy policy.

## What the web host sees

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub automatically records basic technical data when a page loads, such as your IP address, to deliver the page and protect its service. GitHub handles that under the GitHub privacy statement. It never includes your files or anything you type into the tool, and we do not receive it.
